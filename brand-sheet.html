Utkal Vantage

A sunrise made of dots. The mark, wordmark, colors, and type that identify Utkal Vantage across campaigns, merchandise, and client work.

01 · Primary mark

The sunrise

The sun rises over a fine dotted horizon. Each dot is a unit of reach: the sun is built from the same grid as the wordmark, so the logo works as one system.

02 · Construction

Built on a grid

The sun sits on a 13-by-7 dot grid. Dots near the core are larger, dots toward the rim are smaller, and the horizon is a single row of fine dots at half opacity.

03 · Logo versions

Four versions, one system

Use the primary logo by default. Switch to the mark alone where space is tight, the one-color version for print and single-color items, and the badge for avatars and app icons.

Coral is the only brand color. It carries the sun and the accent dot. Ink and paper do the rest. Amber is reserved for highlights in data and charts.

07 · Misuse

Keep the mark intact

The dot grid is the identity. Changing its proportions, angle, or color breaks recognition.

Don't stretch

Never distort the proportions of the sun or wordmark.

Don't rotate

The horizon is always level.

Don't recolor in multiple hues

The sun is coral. The accent dot is the only second color.

Don't add effects

No shadows, glows, or gradients on the mark.
