Every field submission passes through two hardware locks: first, our Haversine Geofence Engine verifies that the promoter's GPS coordinates are within 100m of the assigned canopy anchor (or 20m for bank merchant storefronts). Second, the Photo Audit Engine burns the live GPS coordinates, accuracy radius, Rep ID, and IST timestamp directly into the HTML5 canvas pixel buffer and computes an immutable FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 digest. Any 1-pixel edit triggers an immediate hash mismatch alert.
The Field Promoter Terminal is built on an offline-first IndexedDB + localStorage failover queue. When cellular connectivity drops in dense haats or basement commercial markets, leads and SHA-256 photo proofs are queued locally on the device and automatically flushed to the Ops Console and Client Portal the moment connectivity returns.
Our DualVerificationMachine cryptographically binds the 6-digit OTP verification token to the exact 10-digit Indian mobile number entered on the form, with a 90-second expiration window and a 3-attempt brute-force lockout. If the promoter attempts to edit the phone number field after OTP verification, the state machine immediately revokes verification and locks the submit button.

Every field interaction enforces five statutory safeguards in code: (1) DPDP Act 2023 (Sec 6 & 7): Mandatory Bilingual Consent Notice (English & ଓଡ଼ିଆ) and cryptographic Consent Artifact logged before OTP generation, plus a 1-click Right to Erasure purge tool in the Ops Console. (2) Aadhaar Act & RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025: Active DLP Firewall blocks any 12-digit Aadhaar number, PAN card number, or payment card from being entered on field devices. (3) Anti-Theft Device Masking: Customer phone numbers are masked (+91 94••••••01) on the promoter's phone after submission so field reps cannot scrape lead lists. (4) TRAI TCCCPR Digital Consent Acquisition: Logs explicit DND-override timestamp and authorized channels.
